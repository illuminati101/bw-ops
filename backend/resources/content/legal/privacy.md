## 2. Personal information we collect

**From clients and account holders**

- **Identity and contact details:** your name, mobile number, email address and, for companies, company details and authorised bookers.
- **Verification information:** a copy of your identity document or passport and a photograph (selfie), so that we can confirm who you are before your first booking.
- **Booking information:** services booked, dates and times, pickup, drop-off and stop locations, requirements (for example an armed operator or armoured vehicle), notes you give us, and the name, mobile number and, if you choose to provide it, the photograph of any principal you book for.
- **Location information:** with your permission, your device's location while you are booking or during an active booking, so that your operator can find you and you can share your trip. We do not track your location when you are not booking or on a booking.
- **Payment information:** a token representing your saved card, the card type, last four digits and expiry date. Your full card number is captured and stored by our payment service provider, never by us.
- **Communications:** in-app chat messages, emails, calls and complaints.
- **Safety information:** SOS alerts, including your location at the time, and the emergency contacts you choose to give us.
- **Ratings and feedback** you give after a booking.
- **Technical information:** device type, operating system, app version, push notification tokens, IP address and log data, used to run and secure the Services.

**From operators and job applicants**

Identity and contact details, PSIRA registration, firearm competency and licence details, driving licences and professional driving permits, training certificates, vehicle documents, banking details for payouts, live location while on duty, and job, rating and earnings records. Job applicants give us their CV and certificates.

**About principals and emergency contacts**

If you give us someone else's personal information, such as a principal or an emergency contact, you confirm that you have their permission to do so and that you have told them about this policy.

## 4. Who sees your information

We share personal information only on a need-to-know basis.

- **Operators** assigned to your booking see a first name or code name, the principal's photograph (if provided) for identification, pickup and route details and job notes, from assignment until the booking ends. After that, the booking disappears from their app. Operators never see your phone number, email address, identity document or payment details. Calls and chats run through our platform.
- **Our staff** see the information they need for their role (for example, dispatchers see bookings; finance staff see invoices). Access is controlled by role, protected by two-factor authentication and logged.
- **Service providers** who process information on our behalf under written agreements that require them to keep it confidential and secure:
  - Paystack (card payments);
  - Twilio (SMS and phone calls);
  - Google (maps, address search and route calculation);
  - our hosting provider (servers located in South Africa);
  - email delivery providers.
- **Authorities:** the South African Police Service, emergency medical services, PSIRA or other authorities, where the law requires it, where it is necessary to respond to an emergency or SOS alert, or to protect the rights, property or safety of any person.
- **People you choose:** contacts you share a live trip link with, and your emergency contacts if you raise an SOS alert.
- **A buyer or successor** of our business, under the same protections, if we sell or restructure the business.

We do not sell personal information.

## 5. Cross-border transfers

Our servers are located in South Africa. Some service providers, such as Twilio and Google, may process limited information (for example, a phone number to send an SMS, or an address to calculate a route) outside South Africa. We transfer personal information across borders only in line with section 72 of POPIA, where the recipient is subject to laws, binding corporate rules or agreements that provide an adequate level of protection.

## 7. How we protect it

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, damage and unauthorised access, as required by section 19 of POPIA. These include:

- encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encryption of sensitive fields at rest;
- role-based staff access with two-factor authentication;
- audit logs of every administrative change;
- need-to-know operator access that expires when the job ends;
- regular backups;
- card tokenisation through a PCI DSS-certified payment provider.

If we have reasonable grounds to believe your personal information has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by section 22 of POPIA.

## 8. Your rights

- ask whether we hold personal information about you and request a copy of it;

- withdraw consent where we rely on consent, without affecting processing that already took place;
- not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal consequences for you;
