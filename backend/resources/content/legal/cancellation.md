# Cancellation and No-Show Policy

**Effective date:** {{legal.effective_date}}

When you book with {{company.trading_name}}, we reserve trained operators and vehicles for you and turn other work away. This policy explains when a fee applies if a booking is cancelled or you don't arrive. It forms part of our [Terms of Service]({{company.website}}/legal/terms).

Fees under this policy are reasonable charges for the time and resources we reserve, as permitted by section 17 of the Consumer Protection Act. **No fee applies if you cannot honour a booking because the principal has died or been hospitalised.** We may ask for reasonable proof.

## 1. Advance bookings

You can cancel an advance booking at any time in the app, on the website or by calling us. The fee depends on how much notice you give before the booked start time:

{{cancellation.tiers_table}}

The percentage is calculated on the confirmed quote for the booking. For multi-day and recurring details, it is calculated on the shifts that fall within the notice period.

## 2. On-demand bookings

- **Free:** within {{cancellation.on_demand_free_minutes}} minutes of requesting, or at any time before an operator accepts the job.
- **After an operator accepts:** the service's call-out fee.
- **After the operator arrives:** the no-show terms in section 3 apply.

## 3. No-shows

A booking counts as a no-show if:

- the operator arrives at the pickup point at the booked time and cannot make contact with you or the principal within **{{cancellation.no_show_wait_minutes}} minutes**, despite trying to reach you through the app and our control room; or
- you cancel after the operator has arrived.

The no-show fee is **{{cancellation.no_show_percent}}% of the quote, with a minimum of {{cancellation.no_show_minimum}}**. Each no-show is recorded as a strike on your account (see section 6).

If you are running late, message your operator in the app or call our control room. We will wait where we can, and the waiting time is charged at the booking's overtime rate rather than treated as a no-show.

## 4. Reconfirmation

For advance bookings, we ask you to reconfirm in the app or by replying to our message **{{booking.reconfirm_hours}} hours** before the start time. If you do not reconfirm, our team will try to contact you. If we cannot reach you, we may release the booking. A released booking is treated as a cancellation at the time it is released.

## 5. How fees are collected

- Fees are charged to the card saved on your account. We will always tell you the amount before or when we charge it.
- For team bookings, multi-day details and bookings over {{payments.hold_threshold}}, we place a temporary hold for the no-show fee when the booking is confirmed. If no fee is due, the hold is released after the booking ends. The time it takes to disappear from your statement depends on your bank.
- For approved company accounts on EFT terms, fees are added to your next invoice.

## 6. Strikes

- After **{{strikes.approval_threshold}}** strikes, every new booking must be approved by our team before it is confirmed.
- After **{{strikes.block_threshold}}** strikes, we may suspend your account.

Strikes are reviewed by a person, and we may remove a strike if there was a good reason, such as a medical emergency or an error on our side.

## 7. If we cancel

If we cannot provide a confirmed booking, we will tell you as soon as possible and offer an alternative where we can. You will not be charged for a booking we cancel, and any hold is released immediately.

## 8. Changes to a booking

You can ask to change the time, pickup point or requirements of a booking. If we can accommodate the change, we will show you an updated quote to accept. Moving a booking to a later time is treated as a cancellation only if the new time is more than 7 days after the original.

## 9. Questions and disputes

If you believe a fee was charged in error, contact us at {{company.email}} within 30 days. A person will review the booking timeline, including operator location records and messages, and respond within ten business days.
